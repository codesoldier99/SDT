# Annotated Bibliography

## Introductory

1. **Fraden, J. _Handbook of Modern Sensors_**
   - ISBN: 978-3319193021
   - Year: 2015
   - Review: Broad and practical introduction to sensor types and applications.

2. **Morris, A. S., and Langari, R. _Measurement and Instrumentation_**
   - ISBN: 978-0128008843
   - Year: 2015
   - Review: Strong foundation on measurement systems and instrumentation practice.

## Intermediate

1. **Bentley, J. P. _Principles of Measurement Systems_**
   - ISBN: 978-1292230559
   - Year: 2018
   - Review: Clear treatment of system-level measurement design and error sources.

2. **Doebelin, E. O., and Manik, D. N. _Measurement Systems_**
   - ISBN: 978-0073397467
   - Year: 2010
   - Review: Rigorous analysis of dynamic response and uncertainty.

## Advanced

1. **Gardner, J. W., Varadan, V. K., and Awadelkarim, O. O. _Microsensors, MEMS, and Smart Devices_**
   - ISBN: 978-0471861096
   - Year: 2001
   - Review: MEMS-focused reference useful for advanced and project topics.

2. **Nitaigour, P. _LiDAR: Technologies and Systems_**
   - ISBN: 978-1630819408
   - Year: 2020
   - Review: Concise overview of LiDAR architecture and sensing trade-offs.
